# Time Audit app (Lovable)

**Surveys a user on where their 168 hours per week went, compares against goal week and provides chart.**


## The problem
Most people get to the end of the day or week and realize they didnt do what they wanted to do. They skipped a work out or wasted time reacting to slack and email. The first step to course correcting is awareness. This provides that awareness.

## What it does
1. Surveys a user
2. Provides a dashboard of where time was spent
3. compares that against an ideal week
